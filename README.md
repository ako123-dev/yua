# Yua
## Interoperable mobile money for Papua New Guinea

Yua is a digital wallet and mobile money platform designed for Papua New Guinea (PNG) that connects users across multiple banks and financial providers in one interoperable ecosystem. The platform is built to make digital payments simple, accessible, and inclusive for everyday people, especially those who do not have a formal bank account but still need a safe and convenient way to send, receive, store, and spend money.

Yua is inspired by successful digital payment systems such as Alipay, WeChat Pay, and Kenya's M-PESA. It aims to help PNG move toward a cashless society by enabling fast, secure, and low-friction transactions using QR codes, digital wallets, and mobile-first experiences.

---

## About the idea

Papua New Guinea's economy is still heavily reliant on cash, particularly in informal markets and local communities. Many people, especially small vendors and micro-entrepreneurs, operate outside the formal banking system and lack access to financial products that suit their needs. At the same time, existing mobile money solutions are often limited to one provider or bank ecosystem, creating fragmentation and reducing convenience for users who transact across different institutions.

Yua addresses this gap by creating a unified mobile money experience that works across banks and financial networks. Instead of forcing users to stay within one provider, the wallet acts like a shared digital payment layer that connects multiple financial services and allows users to transact seamlessly.

This makes Yua more than just a wallet: it is a financial access platform for underserved communities, small businesses, and everyday consumers.

---

## The problem

Papua New Guinea has a large informal economy made up of small-scale traders, market sellers, transport operators, farmers, and home-based micro-enterprises. These people often:

- operate in cash-based transactions,
- do not have formal bank accounts,
- struggle with recordkeeping and business tracking,
- cannot easily access digital payment systems that fit their needs,
- rely on informal methods for financial transactions, which can be risky and inefficient.

Existing solutions such as BSP's Wantok Wallet and MiBank's MiWallet are useful, but they are still tied to specific bank ecosystems. This limits interoperability and excludes users who transact across banks or operate outside those networks.

PNG needs a payment system that is:

- bank-agnostic,
- mobile-first,
- easy to use for non-bank users,
- accessible to informal vendors,
- designed for small-value daily transactions,
- scalable to support a wider digital economy.

---

## Our solution

Yua is a mobile money wallet that allows users to:

- create a digital wallet without a formal bank account,
- receive and send money securely,
- scan QR codes to pay for goods or services,
- make merchant payments in-person and online,
- manage transaction history and balances,
- track business activity and spending patterns.

The core idea is to bring bank interoperability into a consumer-friendly mobile experience. Users can transact across multiple banks and mobile money providers through a single platform, similar to how Alipay and WeChat Pay have transformed digital payments in China.

This is also designed to support the broader goal of building a cashless economy in Papua New Guinea, where payments can happen quickly, safely, and transparently from a smartphone.

---

## Why Yua is different

### 1. Interoperability across banks
Unlike many existing wallet products that are limited to one institution, Yua is designed to work across multiple banks and payment networks. This reduces fragmentation and improves usability for consumers and merchants.

### 2. No formal bank account required
Yua is designed for people who are excluded from traditional financial services. A user should be able to register, hold funds, receive payments, and transact digitally without needing a conventional bank account.

### 3. Built for everyday PNG transactions
The solution is tailored for real-world PNG use cases such as:

- buying betelnut and cigarettes,
- purchasing fresh produce at market stalls,
- paying for transport,
- paying local service providers,
- transferring money between friends and family,
- making low-value transactions efficiently.

### 4. Built for SME and informal vendors
The wallet is not only for consumers. It also helps informal vendors and micro-business owners manage their operations better through:

- transaction history,
- sales tracking,
- customer payment records,
- business insights,
- cashflow visibility,
- better financial discipline.

### 5. Future-ready for national data and inclusion
In the future, Yua could support national visibility into the informal economy by providing aggregated, privacy-aware transaction insights that help the Government understand economic activity, improve policy planning, and support broader financial inclusion initiatives.

## Product vision

Yua envisions a Papua New Guinea where the majority of everyday transactions can occur digitally, securely, and conveniently without requiring users to rely on cash alone. By making digital payments accessible to both banked and unbanked communities, Yua supports inclusive economic participation and accelerates the shift toward a cashless society.

The product is designed to empower informal vendors, micro-entrepreneurs, and everyday citizens while creating a scalable foundation for future digital financial infrastructure in PNG.

## Why this matters for PNG

PNG has a unique opportunity to leapfrog traditional financial infrastructure by adopting digital-first financial tools. Many communities may not need legacy banking systems in the same way as before; instead, they need accessible mobile financial services that fit their daily realities.

Yua is positioned to help solve several critical challenges:

- financial exclusion,
- fragmented payment systems,
- limited digital transaction access,
- informal business inefficiencies,
- weak transaction records and business insights,
- slow migration toward digital cashless transactions.
